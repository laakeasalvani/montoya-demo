How a pour goes

From estimate
to driveway

Most residential jobs take two to four days of crew time, plus the cure. Here's what happens, in order.

  1. 1

    Free on-site estimate

    We measure, check drainage and grade, and send a written price, usually within 48 hours.

  2. 2

    Tear-out & forms

    Old concrete comes out, the subgrade is shaped, and forms are set to the finished slope.

  3. 3

    Base & steel

    Crushed rock compacted in lifts, then rebar or mesh set on chairs, not left in the dirt.

  4. 4

    Pour & finish

    Placed, screeded, floated and finished: broom, trowel, stamp or exposed aggregate.

  5. 5

    Cure & walk-through

    Walk on it in 24 hours, drive on it in 7 days. We walk the job with you before we call it done.

About Montoya

A family crew out of
Forest Grove

For 23 years, Montoya Construction has poured the driveways, patios and walkways that Washington County families use every day.

We're a family-owned business, and it shows up in how we work. The person who gives you the estimate is part of the family that stands behind the job. We don't hand your project off to whoever is free that week.

Oregon concrete has its own rules. Months of rain mean every slab needs a real base, a real slope and a finish with grip. We plan every pour around that, from the depth of the rock to where the control joints fall.
